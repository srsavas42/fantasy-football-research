# Season-average volume v2 validation

Validation date: 2026-07-17. The evaluation holds out 2022, 2023, and 2024 in
full, fits only earlier seasons, and uses nflverse Week 1 point-in-time rosters.
Each comparison fold used 300 tuning and 300 retained draws in each of two
chains. Named-player metrics exclude the synthetic replacement buckets so they
remain comparable with the accepted v1 evaluation (`n=1,754`).

Volume v2 is retained as the frozen comparison baseline. The promoted
successor and its long-chain acceptance evidence are documented in
[`volume-v3-validation.md`](volume-v3-validation.md).

## Architecture

1. Team plays determine pass attempts and total carries; targets are constrained
   to pass attempts.
2. A Bernoulli/Beta-Binomial hurdle projects any appearance and games active.
3. An all-position conditional snap model converts active games into season snap
   exposure.
4. QB pass allocation combines within-team QB snap workload and pass attempts
   per offensive snap.
5. Target and carry allocation combine snap exposure with lagged per-snap role.
   Targets blend 75% target-per-snap with 25% prior roster share. Carries use an
   any-carry hurdle and both allocators cap unobserved role innovation at 0.5.
6. One QB/RB/WR/TE replacement bucket per team captures volume earned by players
   absent from the Week 1 roster. Historical player labels plus these buckets
   exactly recover every team pass attempt, target, and carry in 2019-2024.

## Rolling holdout results

| Metric | Accepted model | Volume v2 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Player pass MAE | 0.853 | 0.802 | -6.0% |
| Player pass RMSE | 3.119 | 3.007 | -3.6% |
| Player pass CRPS | 0.602 | 0.586 | -2.7% |
| QB pass MAE | 5.901 | 5.548 | -6.0% |
| QB pass RMSE | 8.213 | 7.918 | -3.6% |
| Target MAE | 0.891 | 0.888 | -0.3% |
| Target CRPS | 0.735 | 0.661 | -10.1% |
| Carry MAE | 0.917 | 0.834 | -9.0% |
| Carry CRPS | 0.647 | 0.609 | -5.9% |
| Availability MAE | 0.221 | 0.221 | flat |
| Availability CRPS | 0.151 | 0.149 | -1.1% |

Volume v2 produces offensive snap-share MAE 0.151, CRPS 0.104, and 82.4%
coverage for an 80% interval. QB attempts per snap has MAE 0.095 and CRPS
0.075. Team-volume performance is effectively unchanged, as expected, because
the team model was not structurally changed.

## Acceptance status

The allocation architecture is accepted on predictive performance. Every
300-by-2-chain fit had zero divergences; the final 2024 target, carry, and carry
eligibility components passed the strict diagnostics. Several upstream
high-dimensional components did not consistently clear R-hat < 1.01 at this
shorter comparison budget, so the saved comparison posteriors are not the final
production artifacts. A longer-chain final fit remained before publishing live
projections. A later scoring correction removed smoothing from the reported
posterior availability draws: exact 80% coverage is 88.2%, 93.3%, and 95.4% in
2022-2024. The actual calibration weakness is therefore excessive interval
width, not undercoverage.

Machine-readable reports are stored under
`.cache/season-average-validation/volume-v2/`, including
`aggregate-final.json`.

## Efficiency handoff

The next phase must preserve temporal direction and out-of-fold generation:

```text
efficiency observed through Y-1 -> volume projection for Y
volume posterior for Y + efficiency through Y-1 -> efficiency projection for Y
```

For model training, any projected-volume feature must be generated by a fold
that excluded that player-season. The first efficiency responses should be
catch rate, receiving yards per target, rushing yards per carry, passing yards
per attempt, and touchdown rates with opportunity-weighted partial pooling.

This handoff is implemented and evaluated in
[`efficiency-v1-validation.md`](efficiency-v1-validation.md). Passing and
rushing efficiency improved direct-share challengers, but neither cleared the
matching share-allocation gate. Receiving efficiency and the open pass-play
participation proxy also failed. A later hurdle-specific test did accept lagged
rushing EPA per carry for carry eligibility in volume v3.
